01 What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser when you visit. They are used to remember information between page loads and sessions — for example, to keep you logged in or to remember your language preference.

NutriFell uses cookies responsibly: only what is strictly necessary to make the app work. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or any cookies that follow you across the web.

02 Cookies NutriFell Uses

The following cookies are set by NutriFell directly. They are all classified as strictly necessary — the app cannot function correctly without them.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration Type
nb_token Authentication token. Keeps you logged in between sessions. 7 days Essential
nf_lang Your language preference (English or Georgian). 1 year Preference
nf_cookies_v1 Records that you have acknowledged this cookie notice. 1 year Essential

03 localStorage Data

In addition to cookies, NutriFell stores some data in your browser's localStorage. This is different from cookies — it never leaves your device and is never sent to our servers. It is used entirely for your convenience.

Key Purpose Stored Where
nb_user Cached copy of your profile for fast UI rendering. Your browser only
nf_streak_v1 Your daily login streak count and best streak. Your browser only
nf_recent_v1 Your recently viewed foods (up to 8). Your browser only
nf_views_v1 Rolling 7-day food view log for the "Trending" section. Your browser only
nf_tour_done_v1 Records whether you have completed the feature tour. Your browser only
nf_onboarded_v1 Records whether you have seen the welcome screen. Your browser only
nf_tip_dismiss Records the last day you dismissed the Tip of the Day. Your browser only
nb_chat / nb_quit_chat Local chat history for NutriAI and Quit Coach (avoids re-loading from server). Your browser only

You can clear all localStorage data by clearing your browser's site data or using your browser's developer tools.

04 No Advertising Cookies

NutriFell does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, retargeting scripts, or any analytics tools that monitor your behaviour across the web. We do not share data with advertising networks. We do not display third-party ads.

Some third-party services that NutriFell connects to (such as Google Fonts via their CDN, or Stripe on checkout pages) may set their own cookies. These are governed by those services' own privacy policies. See our Privacy Policy — Third-Party Services for details.

05 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that deleting or blocking essential cookies will prevent you from staying logged in.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

To clear NutriFell-specific localStorage data, open your browser's developer tools (F12), navigate to Application → Local Storage → your domain, and delete the entries listed above.
