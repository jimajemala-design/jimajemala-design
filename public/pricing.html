Can I switch plans later?

Yes — upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time. Changes take effect at your next billing cycle and unused time is prorated.

What happens to my data if I downgrade?

Your fridge, logs, and profile stay intact. You'll simply return to the Explorer plan's limits until you upgrade again.

Is the Free plan really free?

Absolutely. Explorer is free forever — no credit card required. Paid plans just unlock unlimited AI and advanced tracking.

How do I cancel, and what about refunds?

Cancel in two clicks from your account — no email or phone call. You keep paid features until the end of the period you've already paid for, and we don't bill you again after that.
