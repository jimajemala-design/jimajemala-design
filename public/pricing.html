Is everything really free right now?

Yes. During our beta launch, every feature — including everything in Nutritionist and Elite — is unlocked for everyone at no cost. No credit card, no trial timer.

What is the waitlist for?

When we introduce paid plans after beta, waitlist members get first access and early-bird pricing. Joining costs nothing and you can leave anytime — you keep using everything free in the meantime.

Will I be charged when paid plans launch?

Never automatically. Nothing is billed during beta, and we'll only ever charge you if you actively choose to subscribe once paid plans are live.

What happens to my data later?

Your fridge, logs, profile, and recipes stay yours and stay intact regardless of which plan you end up on.
