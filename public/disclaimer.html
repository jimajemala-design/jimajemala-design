01 Not Medical Advice

The nutritional information, food profiles, health benefits, drawbacks, calorie targets, macro splits, meal plans, and all other content displayed by NutriFell is provided for general informational purposes only. It is:

  • Not a substitute for professional medical advice from a licensed doctor
  • Not a substitute for personalised dietary advice from a registered dietitian or nutritionist
  • Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or health condition
  • Not tailored to your individual medical history, current medications, allergies, or clinical needs

The information about individual foods (calories, vitamins, minerals, benefits, drawbacks) is based on published nutritional data for average compositions. Real foods vary by variety, origin, preparation method, and ripeness. All values are approximate.

02 Calorie Calculations Are Estimates

NutriFell calculates calorie targets using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation — a widely used, peer-reviewed formula for estimating Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE). However, these are statistical estimates, not precise measurements.

  • Individual metabolism varies significantly. Two people of the same age, weight, height, and activity level can have very different calorie needs.
  • Factors not captured by any formula — including thyroid function, gut microbiome, insulin sensitivity, sleep quality, and stress — affect calorie balance.
  • The activity multipliers used are general averages. Your actual expenditure may be higher or lower.
  • Weight predictions and completion date estimates are illustrative. Actual progress will differ.

Use calorie targets as a starting point, monitor how your body responds, and adjust with the guidance of a healthcare professional if needed.

03 AI Chat Is General Guidance Only

NutriAI (the AI chat powered by Google Gemini) provides general nutrition information and meal suggestions based on your profile data. It is not a medical professional and cannot:

  • Review your complete medical history or current medications
  • Diagnose nutritional deficiencies or health conditions
  • Account for drug-nutrient interactions
  • Replace a consultation with a registered dietitian

AI systems can make factual errors. Always verify important health information with a qualified professional. Do not make significant dietary changes based solely on AI-generated suggestions.

04 When to Consult a Professional

You should consult a qualified healthcare professional (doctor, registered dietitian, or appropriate specialist) before:

  • Starting a new diet or making significant changes to your eating habits
  • Pursuing aggressive weight loss or weight gain
  • Following any specific meal plan if you have a chronic health condition (diabetes, cardiovascular disease, kidney disease, etc.)
  • Using NutriFell if you are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Using NutriFell if you are under 18 — NutriFell is for adults only; calorie formulas are not validated for adolescents
  • Taking prescription medications that may interact with dietary changes
  • Introducing large amounts of any specific food if you have known allergies or intolerances

05 Eating Disorders

Calorie counting and food tracking tools can be harmful for people with a history of or current eating disorders, including anorexia nervosa, bulimia nervosa, binge eating disorder, orthorexia, and related conditions.

If you have or suspect you have an eating disorder, please seek support from a qualified mental health professional or eating disorder specialist before using calorie tracking features. NutriFell is not designed for clinical therapeutic use.

If you or someone you know is struggling with an eating disorder, contact an appropriate helpline or healthcare provider in your country. Some resources:

  • International: NEDA Helpline (US): 1-800-931-2237
  • UK: Beat Eating Disorders Helpline: 0808 801 0677
  • Europe: EU Eating Disorders Services

06 Emergency Situations

If you are experiencing a medical emergency — including severe chest pain, difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness, severe allergic reaction, or any other life-threatening situation — call your local emergency services immediately (112 in the EU, 911 in the US, 999 in the UK, or your local equivalent). Do not consult NutriFell or any app in a medical emergency.
